How do I get my export file?

ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Data controls
  2. Export data → Confirm
  3. Open the email from OpenAI and download the .zip

Claude

  1. Settings → Privacy → Export data
  2. The email gives a list of links: download conversations-000.zip
  3. Drop that zip here

Gemini

  1. Open Google Takeout
  2. Select only “My Activity” → Gemini Apps, format JSON
  3. Download the .zip

Exports can take from a few minutes to a few days to arrive.

None of these was “ChatGPT hacked and leaked your Aadhaar”. The point is simpler: your chats are stored, and stored data gets out in many ways.


      
Stop your chats being used for AI training

      
A Stanford study (2025) found the big AI companies use chats for training by default. Turn it off yourself. Setting names change over time; if you can't find one, search the app's help for “model training”.

      

        
ChatGPT
  1. Settings → Data controls
  2. Turn off Improve the model for everyone
  3. For one-off sensitive questions use Temporary chat

        
Claude
  1. Settings → Privacy
  2. Turn off Help improve Claude
  3. Delete chats you no longer need

        
Gemini
  1. Open myactivity.google.com/product/gemini
  2. Turn off Gemini Apps Activity (also shown as “Keep activity”)
  3. Set Auto-delete to 3 months

      


    


    
    

      
Ask the company to delete your data

        
Deleting a chat removes it from your screen. This drafts a formal request under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, asking the company to erase its copies.

      
⚖️ Know this first: the rights in Sections 11–14 of the Act come into force on 13 May 2027. Until then companies are not legally bound in India to act, but most already accept such requests.
